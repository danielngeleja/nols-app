This Sales Partner Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between:

1. NoLSAF, operating under the laws of {{NOLSAF_JURISDICTION}}, with its principal office located at {{NOLSAF_ADDRESS}} ("NoLSAF"); and
2. {{PARTNER_LEGAL_NAME}}, of {{PARTNER_ADDRESS}}, holder of identification {{PARTNER_ID_NUMBER}} ("the Partner").

NoLSAF and the Partner are each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

The Partner introduces property owners to NoLSAF products and supports them through onboarding. In return the Partner earns a share of revenue that those properties generate for NoLSAF. This Agreement sets out what the Partner earns, when it is paid, and for how long.

1. Nature of the relationship

1.1 The Partner is an independent contractor. This Agreement does not create employment, partnership in the legal sense, agency beyond what is stated here, or any entitlement to salary, wages, leave or benefits.

1.2 The Partner has no authority to sign anything on behalf of NoLSAF, to vary NoLSAF pricing, to promise a property owner any term not published by NoLSAF, or to collect money from a property owner in the name of NoLSAF.

1.3 The Partner bears their own costs of travel, communication and equipment unless NoLSAF agrees otherwise in writing.

1.4 The Partner is responsible for their own tax registration, statutory filings and payment of taxes arising from commission received under this Agreement. NoLSAF may make any withholding required by law and will provide the relevant record where applicable.

1.5 Nothing in this Agreement guarantees the Partner a minimum number of leads, properties, transactions, bookings, room nights or earnings.

1.6 In this Agreement, "platform" means the NoLSAF systems made available to the Partner; "attributed property" means a property and product attribution approved in the platform; "eligible net revenue" has the meaning in clause 5.4; and "business day" means a day other than Saturday, Sunday or a public holiday in Tanzania.

2. Term

2.1 This Agreement commences on {{STARTS_AT}} and expires on {{EXPIRES_AT}}, a term of {{TERM_DAYS}} days.

2.2 This Agreement does not renew automatically. Renewal is at the discretion of both Parties and is recorded as a new agreement.

2.3 NoLSAF will notify the Partner at sixty, thirty and seven days before expiry.

2.4 Continued use of the workspace after expiry does not renew this Agreement or create a right to commission. If temporary technical access remains visible, the earning rules continue to follow the recorded contract status.

2.5 The Parties may discuss renewal before expiry, but no renewal is effective until a replacement agreement is issued and accepted through the platform.

2.6 If the commencement date is in the future, the Partner may review and accept the Agreement beforehand, but earning access begins only after NoLSAF activation on or after that date.

3. Territory

3.1 The Partner operates in {{TERRITORY}}.

3.2 Territory is not exclusive unless stated expressly in clause 3.3.

3.3 Exclusivity: {{EXCLUSIVITY_TERMS}}

3.4 Territory describes the Partner's principal operating area. It does not prevent NoLSAF, another partner, a property owner or a customer from conducting business in the same area unless clause 3.3 expressly states otherwise.

3.5 The Partner must not describe the territory as owned, reserved or exclusive unless the exclusivity terms expressly permit that representation.

4. Attribution

4.1 The Partner earns on a property only where NoLSAF has verified that the Partner introduced it. Verification is recorded in the NoLSAF platform against the property and the specific product.

4.2 A property may be attributed for NRMS, for the NoLSAF marketplace, or for both. Each product is attributed separately.

4.3 A property and product may be attributed to only one Partner at any time. Where two Partners claim the same property, NoLSAF decides on the evidence recorded in the platform, and its decision is final.

4.4 The Partner must register a prospect in the platform before approaching the owner. A registered prospect is protected for {{LEAD_PROTECTION_DAYS}} days, extended when the Partner records genuine activity such as a call, a meeting, a document received or a proposal sent.

4.5 The Partner may not register a property they own, control, or in which they or a close family member hold a financial interest, without disclosing that interest in writing and obtaining written approval from NoLSAF.

4.6 Attribution evidence may include platform timestamps, referral links, communications, meeting records, property-owner confirmation and other reliable records. The Partner must keep supporting records and provide them when reasonably requested.

4.7 An attribution may be suspended or removed where it was created through error, duplicate entry, misrepresentation, undisclosed conflict of interest or activity that breaches this Agreement. No earned amount will be removed without a recorded reason and reviewable platform entry.

5. What the Partner earns

5.1 NRMS usage share. {{NRMS_COMMISSION_RATE}}% of eligible net NRMS revenue that NoLSAF collects from an attributed property.

5.2 Marketplace revenue share. {{MARKETPLACE_REVENUE_RATE}}% of the eligible net commission that NoLSAF earns on bookings at an attributed property.

5.3 The marketplace share is a percentage of NoLSAF commission, not of booking value. NoLSAF commission on accommodation is a fraction of what a guest pays. The following worked example forms part of this Agreement and the Partner confirms they have read it:

Booking value:                TSh 1,000,000
NoLSAF commission (10%):      TSh   100,000
Tax and processing:           TSh    12,000
Eligible net NoLSAF revenue:  TSh    88,000
Partner rate:                            20%
Partner earning:              TSh    17,600

On a booking of one million shillings the Partner earns seventeen thousand six hundred shillings, not two hundred thousand.

5.4 Eligible net revenue means the amount NoLSAF has actually collected, after deducting applicable tax, payment processing fees, refunds, chargebacks, reversals, and any discount funded by NoLSAF.

5.5 Rates are fixed for the term of this Agreement. A change to NoLSAF published default rates does not alter this Agreement.

5.6 Unless the platform states otherwise, displayed earnings are estimates until the underlying revenue is collected, validated and approved. Only an amount marked available for payout is payable.

5.7 The Partner is not entitled to commission on taxes, payment charges, refunded amounts, promotional credit, complimentary service, internal test activity or revenue that NoLSAF has not retained.

6. When the Partner earns

6.1 Commission arises only when NoLSAF has received the money. An invoice issued, a booking made, or a property signed up does not by itself create an entitlement.

6.2 NRMS is billed on usage. A property in its free trial generates no NRMS revenue and therefore no commission. The trial length is set by NoLSAF and is currently {{TRIAL_DAYS}} days.

6.3 A property that adopts NRMS but does not use it generates no room nights and therefore no commission.

6.4 The Partner earns only while this Agreement is in force. On expiry or termination, entitlement to further commission ends, including on properties the Partner introduced. Commission already accrued before that date is dealt with under clause 8.

6.5 The platform ledger is the primary record of attribution, eligible revenue, reversals, approvals and payouts. The Partner must notify NoLSAF of a suspected error within thirty days after the relevant entry becomes visible.

6.6 NoLSAF may place an earning under review where supporting revenue, attribution or compliance information is incomplete. Review does not create a right to immediate payment.

7. Reversal

7.1 Where money underlying a commission is refunded, reversed or charged back, the commission is reversed.

7.2 If the commission has not yet been paid, it is cancelled. If it has been paid, the amount is offset against future earnings.

7.3 The Partner will be shown the reversal and its reason in the platform. Nothing is deducted silently.

7.4 A reversal may be partial where only part of the underlying payment is refunded, charged back or found ineligible.

7.5 If future earnings are insufficient to recover a paid reversal, NoLSAF may carry the negative balance forward or request repayment, supported by the underlying ledger record.

7.6 The Partner may raise a reversal query through the support channel and should include the ledger reference and supporting evidence. A query does not suspend an undisputed recovery.

8. Payment

8.1 Commission becomes payable after a validation period allowing for refunds and chargebacks, and after NoLSAF approval.

8.2 The Partner requests payment through the platform. The minimum withdrawal is {{MIN_PAYOUT_AMOUNT}}.

8.3 NoLSAF pays to the account the Partner has registered, and issues a receipt.

8.4 Where this Agreement ends by expiry or by notice under clause 10.1, commission already approved and available is paid in the ordinary cycle. Commission still within its validation period at that date {{TERMINATION_PENDING_TREATMENT}}.

8.5 Where NoLSAF terminates for cause under clause 10.2, NoLSAF may withhold unpaid commission connected to the conduct relied on.

8.6 Payment processing time depends on the selected payment method and third-party provider. NoLSAF is not responsible for delay caused solely by incorrect payout details supplied by the Partner or by the receiving provider.

8.7 The Partner must keep payout details accurate and must not ask NoLSAF to pay an account belonging to an undisclosed third party.

9. Conduct

9.1 The Partner will represent NoLSAF products accurately and will not promise an owner any outcome, revenue figure or booking volume that NoLSAF has not published.

9.2 The Partner will not collect any payment from a property owner on behalf of NoLSAF.

9.3 The Partner will not create false prospects, false properties, or artificial usage in order to generate commission.

9.4 The Partner will keep confidential all NoLSAF pricing logic, commission structures, customer records, dashboards and operational information, both during and after this Agreement.

9.5 The Partner will comply with applicable law, including data protection law, in handling owner and guest information.

9.6 NoLSAF names, logos, product designs, platform materials and training resources remain NoLSAF property. The Partner receives a limited, revocable right to use approved materials only for performing this Agreement.

9.7 The Partner will not offer, request or accept a bribe, secret commission, improper benefit or misleading incentive in connection with NoLSAF business.

9.8 The Partner will use personal data only for the relevant introduction or onboarding activity, apply reasonable security, avoid unnecessary copying, and promptly report loss, disclosure or suspected misuse.

9.9 The Partner must not share workspace credentials, bypass access controls, scrape platform data or use confidential information to compete unfairly with NoLSAF or an attributed property.

10. Termination

10.1 Either Party may terminate on {{NOTICE_DAYS}} days written notice.

10.2 NoLSAF may terminate immediately for cause, including breach of clause 4.5 or clause 9, misrepresentation to a property owner, or conduct damaging to NoLSAF or to a property owner.

10.3 On termination the Partner's access to the sales workspace closes, and the Partner will return or delete NoLSAF materials in their possession.

10.4 Clauses 7, 8.4, 8.5, 9.4 and 11 survive termination.

10.5 NoLSAF may temporarily suspend workspace access while investigating suspected fraud, security risk, material misrepresentation or misuse. Suspension is not termination and does not by itself cancel a valid accrued earning.

10.6 On request, the Partner will reasonably assist with an orderly handover of open property onboarding matters, without representing that the Partner remains authorised after termination.

16.3 If the event continues for more than sixty days and materially prevents performance, either Party may terminate by written notice without affecting rights accrued before termination.

17.3 An attempted transfer by the Partner without approval is ineffective and may result in suspension or termination where it creates a security, compliance or payment risk.
