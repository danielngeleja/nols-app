1. Scope of this policy

1.1  This policy governs one thing only: the optional payment capability inside
     NRMS that lets your guests pay your property online, into a payment account
     that belongs to you.

1.2  It explains what that capability is, what NoLSAF does and does not do, what
     you are agreeing to supply, who decides whether your payment account is
     opened, and what happens when something goes wrong.

1.3  It does not replace your NoLSAF service agreement, and it does not replace
     the agreement you will enter into directly with the payment provider. Where
     this policy and one of those agreements cover the same subject, those
     agreements govern the commercial relationship and this policy governs how
     the capability behaves inside NRMS.

1.4  This policy sets no prices. Service fees, provider charges, settlement
     timing and any commission are governed by your NoLSAF service agreement and
     by the provider's own agreement and schedules. Accepting this policy does
     not create, vary or waive any of those terms.

1.5  In this policy, "you" means the property owner or the legal entity that
     operates the property and holds the NoLSAF Owner account. "We" and "NoLSAF"
     mean NoLSAF and the NRMS platform.


2. Definitions

2.1  NRMS is the NoLSAF Property Management System: the software your property
     uses to run its day to day operations. Section 3 lists what it does.

2.2  Payment provider (or "provider") means the licensed payment institution
     that actually moves money, holds the merchant account and settles funds to
     you. AzamPay is the first provider available under this policy. Other
     providers may be added later, each under its own agreement.

2.3  Merchant account and wallet mean the payment account the provider opens in
     the name of your business, and into which your guests' payments are paid.
     It is the provider's product, opened under the provider's rules, in your
     business name, not in the name of NoLSAF.

2.4  Application means the package of business details and supporting documents
     you complete in NRMS so that the provider can assess you. An application
     has a version number. Each correction after review creates a new version.

2.5  NoLSAF review means our internal completeness and eligibility check of your
     application. It is not a compliance decision and it is not approval by the
     provider. See section 8.

2.6  Activation means the provider has completed its own compliance review,
     opened your merchant account and confirmed it as live. Only the provider
     can cause activation. See section 9.

2.7  Owner account means your personal NoLSAF login as the owner of the
     property, including the email address and phone number recorded on it.

2.8  Staff account means any other NRMS user at your property: managers,
     receptionists, cashiers, waiters, accountants and any other role.


3. What NRMS does without any payment provider

3.1  NRMS is a property management system. It holds the record of what your
     property sold, to whom, when, at what price, and what is still owed. That
     record is the business truth. A payment provider is a separate thing: it is
     a rail that moves money. NRMS does not become a payment company by talking
     to one.

3.2  The following NRMS capabilities do not require any payment provider, and
     continue to work in full whether or not you ever subscribe to online
     payment:

     (a)  reservations, availability, rates, room inventory, check in, check
          out, and guest profiles;
     (b)  room folios, guest folios, group folios, agent folios and master
          folios;
     (c)  restaurant, bar, room service, transport, minibar, laundry and other
          operational charges;
     (d)  restaurant and bar ordering, room and table QR ordering, stock and
          order fulfilment;
     (e)  cash settlement and cashier shift controls;
     (f)  your existing external bank details, mobile money till, card terminal
          or other payment instructions, where you have configured them;
     (g)  recording payments you received outside NRMS, under the existing
          evidence and audit controls;
     (h)  night audit, property ledger, reports, and eligible fiscal receipt
          workflows;
     (i)  payment reminders and instructions, provided they do not claim to be
          initiated or verified by a provider.

3.3  This list is the boundary of what you keep in every circumstance described
     in this policy. Nothing in section 10 or section 11 removes it.


4. What the payment capability does

4.1  When your merchant account is active, NRMS can present your guest with an
     online way to pay, and can then record the result against the correct
     booking, folio or outlet order automatically.

4.2  The payment purposes this capability is intended to support are:

     (a)  a deposit, a balance, or a full payment for a direct accommodation
          booking;
     (b)  the same, taken at the front desk for a reservation;
     (c)  a room folio balance that combines room, restaurant, bar and service
          charges;
     (d)  a contracted agency or master folio balance;
     (e)  a restaurant or bar order paid by a walk in guest;
     (f)  a restaurant or bar order paid immediately by a resident guest;
     (g)  room service charged to a room and paid with the folio;
     (h)  transport or another property service, paid directly or charged to a
          folio.

4.3  For all of the above, the recipient is your approved merchant account, or,
     where you have explicitly contracted a separate outlet merchant, that
     outlet's approved merchant account.

4.4  NRMS decides the destination of a payment on the server, before the payment
     starts, and records that decision. A guest may choose an offered payment
     channel. A guest can never choose, supply or influence the merchant or
     wallet that receives the money.

4.5  Every payment attempt is recorded under a single NRMS payment intent, with
     the provider and provider account that were used at the time. Changing
     provider later never rewrites what already happened.

4.6  If a provider is unavailable or rejects a request, NRMS does not silently
     re-route an uncertain or still processing payment to a different provider.
     Doing so risks charging your guest twice. NRMS stops and reports instead.

4.7  A payment account belongs to the registered company, not to a single
     property. Where one company operates several properties, those properties
     share one application, one review and one payment account, and each
     property's payments are still recorded against that property in NRMS. A
     property that trades under a different registered company, with its own
     TIN, needs its own application and gets its own payment account.


5. What it never does

These are hard boundaries. They are built into the system, not just stated here.

5.1  NoLSAF does not hold your money. Guest payments for your property are paid
     into your merchant account with the provider. NoLSAF is not the recipient,
     not the custodian, and not an escrow agent for those funds.

5.2  NoLSAF is not a bank, and is not a licensed payment service provider. It
     does not issue accounts, hold balances or settle funds. The provider does.

5.3  There is no fallback to a NoLSAF account. If your merchant account is not
     active, is misconfigured, or is temporarily unavailable, the payment fails
     with a clear message. It is never redirected to NoLSAF and collected on
     your behalf.

5.4  Payments owed to NoLSAF are kept separate from payments owed to you, at
     every level: the data, the provider reference, the ledger and the reports.
     An NRMS subscription or service fee is a different payment purpose from a
     guest paying for a stay, and the two are never mixed.

5.5  This capability does not cover payroll, supplier payments, withdrawals from
     your wallet, changes to your bank account, or any other disbursement. Those
     need their own authorisation and their own provider contract, even where a
     provider happens to offer them.

5.6  Subscribing to NRMS does not subscribe you to online payment. Creating a
     draft application does not open a wallet, does not submit anything to a
     provider, and cannot accept money.

5.7  Approval by a NoLSAF administrator is not activation, and cannot become
     activation. See sections 8 and 9.


6. The account security contact

6.1  The provider needs an email address and a phone number for the merchant
     account. Those two contacts are not ordinary business details. They are the
     credentials of last resort: they receive sign in verification, they are
     used to recover the account, and they are used to confirm instructions that
     move money.

6.2  For that reason NRMS does not let anyone type them into the application
     form. They are taken from your Owner account, and only after that email
     address and that phone number have been verified.

6.3  If either is unverified, the application cannot be submitted. Verify them
     in My Profile first. This is deliberate and there is no way around it.

6.4  Your staff may hold NRMS accounts, may operate the property, and may
     receive operational notifications about payments. Your staff may not be the
     owner of the provider account, may not be its verification destination, may
     not be its recovery contact, and may not control the wallet. Those roles
     belong to the Owner account only.

6.5  If your own email address or phone number changes, update and re-verify it
     on your Owner account. The application uses the verified values as they
     stand when you submit.

6.6  Keep control of your Owner account. Anyone who controls it, and who can
     receive its verification messages, is in a position to affect where your
     money goes.


7. What you supply

7.1  You supply, in the application form:

     (a)  the registered legal name of the business, exactly as it appears on
          the company registration;
     (b)  the trading name, if it differs;
     (c)  the company registration number;
     (d)  the company Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN);
     (e)  the country of registration.

7.2  The TIN you enter must match the Company TIN already saved on your Owner
     Workspace profile. NRMS checks this and will not let you submit a
     mismatched TIN. This stops one owner account from being used to open a
     merchant account for a different business.

7.3  You supply, as evidence, the documents already held on your NoLSAF profile:

     (a)  a current business licence;
     (b)  a TIN certificate.

     NRMS links to the copies you have already uploaded rather than asking you
     for them again. A document that is missing, expired or previously rejected
     counts as not supplied.

7.4  When you submit, NRMS records which specific document uploads formed the
     package, and the state each was in at that moment. If you later replace a
     document on your profile, that does not silently change what a reviewer
     approved.

7.5  When you submit, NRMS also freezes the details in your application and
     stores a fingerprint of them. This is what allows anyone later to prove
     that what was sent to the provider is exactly what was reviewed and
     approved, unaltered.

7.6  You confirm that the information and documents are accurate, current, and
     genuinely yours to supply, and that you are authorised to accept this
     policy for the business named in the application.

7.7  By accepting this policy you consent to NoLSAF transmitting your
     application, including the identity information and supporting documents
     described above, to the payment provider for the purpose of assessing and
     opening your merchant account.

7.8  We record who accepted this policy, which version, at what time, and the
     network address and browser the acceptance came from. That record is kept
     as evidence of consent.


8. NoLSAF review and approval

8.1  A NoLSAF administrator reviews a complete application before anything is
     sent to the provider.

8.2  That review checks completeness, internal consistency and eligibility. It
     is not a compliance decision, not a creditworthiness decision, and not a
     decision the provider is bound by in any way.

8.3  The administrator may:

     (a)  return the application for correction, with a reason. Correcting it
          creates a new version, so the version the reviewer saw stays intact;
     (b)  reject the application, with a reason;
     (c)  approve the application, which queues it for submission to the
          provider and does nothing else.

8.4  An approval never activates a merchant account, never opens a wallet and
     never enables checkout. The system cannot record an active account as a
     result of an internal approval, by any route.

8.5  An administrator who also administers the merchant cannot decide on that
     merchant's own application.

8.6  Every decision is recorded with the administrator, the time, the reason and
     the state before and after. You are notified of the outcome.

8.7  Once an application is approved, its business details are final. The legal
     name, trading name, registration number, TIN and country cannot be changed
     from your workspace at any point after that, because they are the package
     the provider was given and assessed. The same applies to an application
     that was rejected. If any of those details is wrong, or changes later,
     contact NoLSAF support so it can be handled through a controlled process
     with the provider rather than by editing an approved record.


9. Provider review and activation

9.1  The provider performs its own compliance and risk review, under its own
     rules and legal obligations.

9.2  The provider alone decides whether to open, restrict, refuse, suspend or
     close your merchant account and wallet. NoLSAF cannot overrule that
     decision and does not attempt to.

9.3  Your merchant account becomes active in NRMS only when the provider
     confirms it, with the identifiers that payment routing needs. Until then,
     online payment stays off for your property.

9.4  The provider may ask for corrections or further information at any stage,
     including after activation.

9.5  Where the provider requires a direct agreement with you, that agreement
     governs your relationship with the provider, including how and when funds
     are settled to you.

9.6  NoLSAF may act as platform administrator with the provider in order to
     orchestrate payments, support you, reconcile transactions, apply risk
     controls and report. That role is for those purposes. It does not make
     NoLSAF the recipient of your money, its custodian, or an unrestricted
     controller of your account.


10. Refunds, reversals and disputes

10.1 A refund or a reversal is a separate, recorded event. It never erases the
     original payment, and it never rewrites history.

10.2 Cancelling or voiding an unpaid order is an operational action and does not
     create a refund. Voiding an order that was paid must create an auditable
     refund or reversal.

10.3 Refunds are executed by the provider and are subject to what that provider
     supports. Some providers do not support partial refunds. Where a capability
     is not supported, NRMS disables it rather than pretending it works.

10.4 Guest disputes and chargebacks, where the provider's rules allow them, are
     handled under the provider's process. You are responsible for the goods and
     services you sold and for responding with evidence.

10.5 You remain responsible for your own refund and cancellation terms towards
     your guests. NRMS records and executes them; it does not set them.


11. Suspension and withdrawal

11.1 You may stop using the payment capability. Doing so removes only that
     capability.

11.2 If the provider rejects, restricts or suspends your merchant account, the
     effect is confined to that provider account and the channels that depend on
     it. It does not suspend your property, does not erase historical payments,
     does not block cash or manual settlement, and does not disable unrelated
     NRMS services.

11.3 A provider outage does not stop restaurant service, folio posting, front
     desk work or cash collection. Payments already in progress remain protected
     against duplicate charging.

11.4 NoLSAF may suspend the payment capability for your property where it is
     required to by law or by the provider, where there is credible evidence of
     fraud or misuse, or where continuing would put guests' money at risk. We
     will tell you the reason unless we are legally prevented from doing so.

11.5 No owner is automatically enrolled in, migrated to, or forced onto a
     provider. Enabling one adds capability. Disabling one removes only that
     capability.


12. Your obligations

12.1 Supply accurate information and genuine documents, and keep them current.

12.2 Replace documents before they expire, and resolve anything a reviewer or
     the provider returns to you.

12.3 Keep your Owner account email and phone verified, and keep control of both.

12.4 Do not use the capability to collect money for a business other than the
     one named in the approved application.

12.5 Comply with the law that applies to you, including tax and fiscal receipt
     obligations, and with the provider's rules.

12.6 Make sure your staff understand that operational access to NRMS is not
     control of the payment account.


13. Data, records and privacy

13.1 The identity information and documents described in section 7 are sent to
     the provider for the purpose of opening and maintaining your merchant
     account, and for no other purpose.

13.2 NRMS records the state of your application, the decisions taken on it, the
     documents linked to it and the acceptance of this policy. These records are
     kept for as long as we are required to keep them, and to resolve disputes.

13.3 Payment data is handled under the provider's rules and ours. NRMS stores
     the references it needs to reconcile a payment. It does not store your
     guests' card numbers or wallet credentials.

13.4 Every meaningful action on an application is written to an audit trail with
     the actor, the time and the reason.


14. Changes to this policy

14.1 This policy is versioned. Your acceptance is bound to the version number
     and to a fingerprint of this exact text.

14.2 If we change the text, the version changes with it. Your previous
     acceptance is retained as a record but is marked as superseded, and you
     will be asked to read and accept the new version.

14.3 If the policy is updated while you have the page open, your acceptance will
     be refused and you will be shown the current version. This is intentional:
     you cannot be recorded as having accepted terms you were not shown.
